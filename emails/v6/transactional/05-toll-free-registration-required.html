Due to new rules and requirements, Toll Free Messaging aggregators are blocking all restricted ('unverified') Toll-Free Numbers from sending text messages. Because you have secured a brand new LinkedPhone Toll Free Number, your number is currently considered 'unverified'.

What do I need to know?

Toll Free Numbers are considered restricted if the number has not yet been submitted for verification OR if a previously verified number has had its verification revoked.

The toll-free verification process ensures that your Toll Free Number complies with carrier policies and industry best practices. This also provides the Toll-Free Messaging Aggregator with relevant service information to reduce the likelihood of false positive filtering and wrongful SPAM blocks.
