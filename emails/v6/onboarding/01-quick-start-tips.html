Quick-Start Tips

 

OK, let's get you up and running. The following are a few important pointers you should understand while setting up your LinkedPhone business phone system. Please take a few minutes to go through these.

 

Your Greeting

This is what your callers will hear when they call your LinkedPhone number. For example:

 

"Hello! Thank you for calling Jetsetter Realty. For Sales, press 1. For Technical Support, press 2. For Billing, press 3. For hours and location, press 4. To speak with our receptionist, press 0. Or, dial the extension of the person you wish to reach."

If you haven't figured out what your call menu will look like at this time, no problem. Just add a temporary greeting and come back to this step once everything else is set up.

Call Menu Options

LinkedPhone enables you set up a professional corporate quality call menu quickly and easily. Your call menu acts as a 'Virtual Receptionist' to handle all of your incoming calls. With LinkedPhone, easily provide callers with the following call options.

 
  • Route calls to an individual or an entire team. You can route your calls to any cell phone, home phone, or IP / SIP phone (the desk phone we see in most office environments) easily.
  • Play a recorded message with store hours, location, or company events.
  • Transfer to company directory. The company directory is automatically created when you add colleagues to your phone system.
  • Transfer to voicemail. Voicemails are sent to you by email, including the audio file and free voicemail transcription (a written version of the voicemail).
  • Transfer to sub-menu. Menu options for, for example, a customer service department with its own set of menu options.

Let's talk about call routing specifically. Let's say you would like to set up your call menu such that when a caller presses 3, she is routed to your billing department. At your company, Kristy and Michael handle all billing questions. Kristy travels a lot and would like to receive billing questions on her cell phone. Michael works from home and would like to receive calls on his home phone. All you need to do is create call menu option 3, give it an intuitive name like 'Billing Questions', and add Kristy's cell phone and Michael's home phone as the routing destinations. That's it!

Each user may also route calls to multiple devices. Simply add each device to the call menu option settings. For example, you might want both your cell and desk phones to ring simultaneously during business hours. Alternatively, you may want your desk phone to receive calls during the day and your cell phone at night. In this case, simply set your cell phone to "Disabled" in the call menu option while in the office and set it to "Enabled" when you leave. It's that easy!

Some of our clients have a receptionist on their team. If you'd like to send calls to him or her, simply add a call menu option to route the caller to the 'operator' or 'receptionist' when he presses 0 or whatever number you prefer.

One last thing on this topic. LinkedPhone has many clients who are entrepreneurs. They love LinkedPhone because they can set up various call menu options for 'Customer Service', 'Technical Support', etc. and have all those calls routed to themselves. The entrepreneur is able to leave a very polished and professional image while she builds her business!

Voicemail

Voicemails are sent by email. Everyone loves this because you can save, download, file, forward, and access them on the go without commingling them with your personal voicemails. When you set up your call menu, know that each menu option comes with its own voicemail box. Simply list the recipient email addresses for that option and you're all set! LinkedPhone sends both written and audio versions of the voicemail. Yay!

 

Your Business Hours

LinkedPhone enables you to provide different call menus for business hours and for closed hours. Any cell, home, or desk phone that has been linked to your LinkedPhone account will ring only during business hours. For this reason, it is important that you set your business schedule in our app or on the web so that your phone system works as expected.

 

By default, when a caller dials your LinkedPhone number outside of business hours they are sent to voicemail. However, you can set up how calls are handled anyway you want. Create a different greeting. Send callers to an off-hours operator. Send callers to a colleague in a different time zone. It's up to you! For example:

"Thank you for calling Jetsetter Realty. Our offices are currently closed. We are open Monday through Friday from 8am to 7pm. Please press 0 to speak with the operator. Otherwise, please leave a message and we'll get back to you as soon as possible."
