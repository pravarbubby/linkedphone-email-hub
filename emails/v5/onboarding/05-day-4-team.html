What do team members have access to?

Customer interactions are shared, including calls, texts, contacts, and tickets. Team members do not have access to admin controls like Auto Attendant and other business-level settings.

 

What if a team member is off?

Have the team member set their status to "Offline" in the app. If they use their phone's Do Not Disturb or Airplane Mode instead, their personal voicemail can answer the business call before it reaches the rest of your team.
