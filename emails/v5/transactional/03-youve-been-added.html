If you're using LinkedPhone on your mobile device, in rare circumstances, your business calls may be incorrectly delivered to your personal voicemail box (i.e. the one provided by your carrier). This happens because our systems misinterpret your personal voicemail greeting for a live person and routed the caller to your personal voicemail.

If you're experiencing this scenario, please update your personal voicemail greeting (the one on your cell phone) with one that satisfies each of the following: 1) is at least four seconds long 2) has minimal silence at the beginning of the greeting and in between words 3) has minimal background noise. If this doesn't solve the issue for you, please email support@linkedphone.com. One of our kind Partner Success Associates will quickly get you up and running.
