Voicemail Greetings

Set up two voicemail greetings - one for open hours and one for closed hours. When your business is open and your team is busy, the "Open Hours" voicemail greeting is played. It should say something like "You've reached us during business hours but no one is available to take your call..." When your business is closed, your "Closed Hours" voicemail greeting is played and should say something like "We are currently closed. Please leave a message..."
