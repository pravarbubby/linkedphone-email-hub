Professional greetings tell callers they’ve reached the right place. Let’s set up the three greetings every business should have. Your Welcome Message plays before we ring your devices. Voicemail greetings play only when no one answers.

 
1
 

Intro Greeting

The first thing callers hear. It's off by default, so calls ring straight through. Turn it on to play an intro first.

“Thanks for calling {{business_name}}. Please hold while we connect your call.”

2
 

Business Hours Voicemail Greeting

Let callers know you’re unavailable and you’ll return their call soon.

“Thanks for calling {{business_name}}. We're helping other customers right now. Leave a message and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.”

3
 

After Hours Voicemail Greeting

Let callers know you’re closed, when you’ll reopen, and when they can expect a callback.

“Thanks for calling {{business_name}}. We're closed right now. We're open Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm. Leave a message and we'll return your call the next business day.”

 

Why two voicemail greetings?

A caller at 2pm and a caller at 10pm need different information. One voicemail greeting can't do both jobs.
