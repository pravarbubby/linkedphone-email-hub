By now, you’ve got the basics down. But there’s a lot more LinkedPhone can do to save you time, keep you organized, and help you take better care of customers. Here are a few features worth knowing about:

 

Automate the Busywork

 

Auto Replies

Can’t respond right away? Automatically send a text when you miss a call or receive a new message, so customers know you’ll get back to them.

 

AI Reply Suggestions

Need help finding the right words? Get AI-powered suggestions for text replies based on the context of the conversation, then review, edit, or send.

 

Lisa, Your AI Receptionist (Beta)

Lisa can answer calls, talk with customers, handle common questions, capture information, and route callers to the right person on your team.

 

AI Call Summaries

Skip the note-taking. LinkedPhone can summarize your calls so you can quickly remember what was discussed and what happens next.

Call Handling

 

Call Recording

Record business calls when you need a clear record of what was said.

 

Call Transfer

Send a live call to another team member when they’re the right person to help. Your customer stays connected without having to hang up and call again.

Stay Organized and Grow

 

Tickets

Turn a customer request into a ticket when something needs follow-up. Assign it to a team member, add notes and documents, and keep track of what needs to get done.

 

Keep Your Existing Business Number

Already have a number your customers know? Port it to LinkedPhone whenever you’re ready and keep using it for business calls and texts.

 

Additional Phone Numbers

Need another number for the same business or a different one? Add more business numbers without adding another phone.

 
🎉You don’t need to use everything at once. Start with the features that solve a problem for your business today, and come back to the others when you need them.
