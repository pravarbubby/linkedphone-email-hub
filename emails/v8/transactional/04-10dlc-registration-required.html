Dear Valued Customer,

 

What's happening?

 

Recently, the text messaging aggregators that LinkedPhone uses have started blocking unregistered numbers from sending outbound text messages. This change is part of a new initiative by Mobile Network Operators (MNOs) like AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon, known as 10DLC (10 Digit Long Code). If your brand is not registered, your messages could be filtered or blocked.

 

What do I need to know?

 

The requirement to register your number is aimed at enhancing the security and reliability of text messaging services. This also helps the Messaging Aggregator reduce the likelihood of false positives and wrongful SPAM blocks. Please note that this is a universal requirement for all virtual phone number service providers.
