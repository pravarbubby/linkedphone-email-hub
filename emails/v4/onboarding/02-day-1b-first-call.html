Should I import my contacts into LinkedPhone?

That’s completely up to you. Giving LinkedPhone access to your phone’s contacts lets you see familiar names instead of strings of numbers. Importing contacts is different: it adds customers, vendors, and other business contacts to LinkedPhone, so you and your team can easily find, call, or text them from your business number.
