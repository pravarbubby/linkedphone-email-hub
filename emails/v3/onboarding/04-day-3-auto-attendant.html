Your greetings welcome callers. Auto Attendant sends every call to the right place, like a receptionist that never takes a break. It’s an easy way to give your business a polished, professional phone experience without the cost and complexity of a traditional business phone system.

 

Here are three things to know:

1

Phone Menu

Setting up a Phone Menu is optional. It lets callers choose how to get help. For example, “Press 1 for Sales. Press 2 for hours and location. Press 3 for Customer Service.” From there, calls route to any person or team at your business.

2

Play Business Information

Not every caller needs to speak with someone. Your Phone Menu can also play recorded information like business hours, locations, upcoming events, or current promotions.

3

Team Directory

Let callers choose who they want to speak with by adding a Team Directory to your Phone Menu. Your directory is created automatically from your team members, so there’s nothing extra to set up.

 

It’s that easy to make your small business sound polished and professional.

 

Do I have to update my Intro Greeting if I set up a Phone Menu?

Yes. Include your Phone Menu options in your Intro Greeting so callers know what to press.
